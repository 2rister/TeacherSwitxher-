**Aim:** Review and practise the four tenses, recognise time-marker words, and use the tenses correctly in writing and speaking.

## Part 1 — Grammar Review: Present Simple vs. Present Continuous (15 minutes)

### Present Simple
**Use:** habits, routines, facts, permanent situations, schedules.

**Form:**
- I / You / We / They **work** → *don't work* → *Do ... work?*
- He / She / It **works** → *doesn't work* → *Does ... work?*

**Marker words:** always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day/week/year, on Mondays, once a week

**Examples:**
- She **works** in a hospital.
- I **don't drink** coffee.
- **Does** he **speak** French?
- The train **leaves** at 9 a.m. every morning.

### Present Continuous
**Use:** actions happening now, temporary situations, fixed future arrangements.

**Form:** subject + **am/is/are** + verb-**ing**

**Marker words:** now, right now, at the moment, today, this week/month, look!, listen!

**Examples:**
- I **am studying** English right now.
- They **aren't watching** TV at the moment.
- **Is** she **cooking** dinner now?
- We **are meeting** Tom on Friday. (future arrangement)

### Quick Contrast
| Present Simple | Present Continuous |
|---|---|
| I **play** tennis every Sunday. | I **am playing** tennis now. |
| He **works** in a bank. | He **is working** on a report today. |

## Practice 1 (20 minutes)

### Exercise 1 — Circle the correct form
1. She usually (walks / is walking) to school.
2. Look! It (rains / is raining) outside.
3. We (don't watch / aren't watching) TV every night.
4. He (doesn't work / isn't working) at the moment — he's on holiday.
5. My parents (visit / are visiting) us every summer.

### Exercise 2 — Fill in the gaps with the correct form (present simple or present continuous)
1. Tom usually _______ (get up) at 7 a.m., but today he _______ (sleep) late.
2. _______ you _______ (like) pizza?
3. Right now, the children _______ (play) in the garden.
4. My sister _______ (not / drink) coffee. She prefers tea.
5. Listen! Someone _______ (knock) on the door.
6. Water _______ (boil) at 100°C.
7. We _______ (study) for our exam this week.
8. He _______ (never / arrive) late.

### Exercise 3 — Write your own sentences
Write 4 sentences: 2 with present simple (a habit and a fact) and 2 with present continuous (something happening now and a future arrangement).

## Part 2 — Grammar Review: Past Simple vs. Past Continuous (15 minutes)

### Past Simple
**Use:** finished actions/events at a specific time in the past.

**Form:**
- Regular verbs: verb + **-ed** (worked, played)
- Irregular verbs: special forms (went, saw, had, did)
- Negative: **didn't** + base verb
- Question: **Did** + subject + base verb?

**Marker words:** yesterday, last night/week/year, ago, in 2010, when I was a child

**Examples:**
- I **watched** a film last night.
- She **didn't go** to school yesterday.
- **Did** you **see** that movie?
- He **went** to Spain two years ago.

### Past Continuous
**Use:** actions in progress at a specific moment in the past; background actions interrupted by another action.

**Form:** subject + **was/were** + verb-**ing**

**Marker words:** while, when, at 8 o'clock yesterday, all day yesterday

**Examples:**
- I **was sleeping** at midnight.
- They **weren't listening** to the teacher.
- **What were** you **doing** when I called?
- She **was cooking** dinner **while** he **was watching** TV.
- The phone **rang** while I **was having** a shower. *(past continuous = background, past simple = interruption)*

### Quick Contrast
| Past Simple | Past Continuous |
|---|---|
| I **finished** my homework at 9 p.m. | I **was doing** my homework at 8 p.m. |
| She **called** me while I **was cooking**. | *(interruption + background)* |

## Practice 2 (20 minutes)

### Exercise 1 — Circle the correct form
1. While I (walked / was walking) home, it started to rain.
2. He (watched / was watching) TV all evening yesterday.
3. They (didn't go / weren't going) to the party last night.
4. What (did you do / were you doing) at 7 p.m. yesterday?
5. She (broke / was breaking) her leg while she (skied / was skiing).

### Exercise 2 — Fill in the gaps with the correct form (past simple or past continuous)
1. When I _______ (arrive) home, my brother _______ (watch) TV.
2. Yesterday I _______ (go) to the market and _______ (buy) some fruit.
3. While she _______ (study), the lights _______ (go) out.
4. We _______ (not / see) each other last year.
5. _______ you _______ (sleep) when the earthquake happened?
6. He _______ (live) in Rome from 2015 to 2018.
7. They _______ (walk) in the park when it _______ (start) to rain.
8. I _______ (not / hear) the phone because I _______ (have) a shower.

### Exercise 3 — Sentence combination
Combine each pair of sentences using **while** or **when** and past simple/past continuous.
1. I was reading a book. My friend called me.
2. The children were playing outside. It started to rain.
3. She was cooking dinner. The fire alarm rang.

## Part 3 — Mixed Review: All Four Tenses (15 minutes)

### Exercise 1 — Choose the correct tense
1. I usually (go / am going) to work by bus, but today I _______ (drive).
2. She _______ (write) a letter when the power _______ (go) out.
3. Look! The dog _______ (chase) the cat!
4. Last summer, we _______ (travel) to Italy.
5. He _______ (not / like) horror films.
6. What _______ you _______ (do) at this time yesterday?
7. My father _______ (work) as an engineer.
8. While I _______ (wait) for the bus, I _______ (see) an old friend.

### Exercise 2 — Short story gap-fill
Complete the story with the correct tense (present simple, present continuous, past simple, or past continuous).

> Every day, Anna _______ (take, present simple) the 8 a.m. train to work. Yesterday, however, she _______ (miss, past simple) it because she _______ (talk, past continuous) to her neighbour when the train _______ (arrive, past simple) at the station. Right now, she _______ (wait, present continuous) for the next train, and she _______ (think, present continuous) about how she can be on time tomorrow. She _______ (never / be, present simple) late for a meeting, so this _______ (be, present simple) a new problem for her.

## Speaking Wrap-up (10 minutes)

In pairs, take turns answering:
1. Describe your typical morning routine (present simple).
2. Describe what is happening in your classroom right now (present continuous).
3. Tell your partner about something interesting you did last weekend (past simple).
4. Tell your partner what you were doing at exactly 10 p.m. last night (past continuous).
